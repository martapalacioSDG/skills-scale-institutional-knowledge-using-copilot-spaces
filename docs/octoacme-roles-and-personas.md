# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder/Client Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder/Client Representatives serve as the voice of the customer and business. They communicate requirements, approve deliverables, and provide feedback that ensures projects align with business objectives and stakeholder expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Communicate business requirements and success criteria
- Approve key deliverables and project gates
- Provide feedback on solutions and progress
- Escalate concerns and blockers to project leadership
- Validate that delivered solutions meet business needs

### Goals
- Ensure project outcomes deliver maximum business value
- Maintain alignment between team delivery and stakeholder expectations
- Enable timely decision-making through clear communication
- Bridge communication gap between business and execution teams

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Works with Project Manager**: Participates in kickoff meetings, status reviews, and approval gates
- **Works with Product Manager**: Validates requirements alignment and provides business context
- **Communicates with Developers**: Clarifies requirements during implementation and participates in demos
- **Collaborates with QA Lead**: Reviews acceptance criteria and validates test scenarios

### Typical Communication
- Requirement gathering sessions and workshops
- Milestone reviews and approval gates
- Feedback and sign-off on deliverables
- Escalation communication when issues impact business outcomes

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and implement quality standards across the project. They establish testing protocols, acceptance criteria, and quality gates to ensure deliverables meet defined standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and acceptance criteria for all work
- Create and execute test plans and testing protocols
- Identify and track quality issues and defects
- Collaborate on test automation and quality metrics
- Report on quality status and risks to project leadership
- Validate that solutions meet acceptance criteria before sign-off

### Goals
- Ensure consistent, high-quality deliverables
- Reduce post-release defects and customer issues
- Provide visibility into quality status throughout the project lifecycle
- Enable continuous improvement through quality metrics

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Reports to Project Manager**: Provides quality status, risks, and escalations
- **Collaborates with Developers**: Reviews code quality, participates in code reviews, and supports test coverage goals
- **Works with Product Manager**: Clarifies acceptance criteria and validates solutions meet requirements
- **Partners with Technical Architect**: Ensures testing strategies align with technical design decisions
- **Communicates with Stakeholders**: Provides assurance that deliverables meet quality expectations

### Typical Communication
- Quality planning and test strategy discussions
- Daily quality status updates and defect reports
- Quality review meetings and gate sign-offs
- Metrics and trend analysis on quality indicators

---

## Security/Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Security/Compliance Officers ensure that projects adhere to security standards, compliance requirements, and risk management practices. They identify security risks, validate compliance controls, and guide the team through secure development and deployment practices.

### Responsibilities
- Assess security risks and vulnerabilities
- Validate compliance with regulatory and organizational standards
- Review security design and architecture decisions
- Guide secure development and deployment practices
- Identify and report security issues and mitigation strategies
- Participate in risk management and incident response

### Goals
- Protect organizational assets and customer data
- Ensure regulatory and compliance requirements are met
- Minimize security risks throughout the project lifecycle
- Enable the team to build secure solutions by design

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Advises Project Manager**: Identifies security risks and compliance dependencies that impact project planning
- **Collaborates with Technical Architect**: Reviews technical design for security controls and compliance
- **Works with Developers**: Provides guidance on secure coding practices and security standards
- **Partners with Quality Assurance**: Ensures security testing is included in test plans
- **Engages Stakeholders**: Communicates compliance status and security risk posture

### Typical Communication
- Security and compliance reviews during planning phase
- Risk registers and threat assessments
- Security design reviews and architecture validation
- Compliance status reporting and audit communications

---

## Technical Architect/Tech Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Architects/Tech Leads make critical technical decisions that shape the design and implementation strategy. They guide the team through complex technical challenges, manage technical dependencies, and ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational technology standards.

### Responsibilities
- Make technical decisions and define architectural approach
- Resolve complex technical challenges and design trade-offs
- Manage technical dependencies and integration points
- Guide developers on best practices and technical standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks
- Support planning and execution phases with technical expertise

### Goals
- Ensure technically sound and scalable solutions
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable efficient development through clear technical guidance
- Manage technical risks proactively throughout the project

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Advises Project Manager**: Identifies technical risks, dependencies, and timeline implications
- **Collaborates with Product Manager**: Ensures technical feasibility of requirements and trade-off discussions
- **Guides Developers**: Provides architectural guidance, design decisions, and technical mentorship
- **Works with Quality Assurance**: Ensures testing strategies align with technical design
- **Partners with Security Officer**: Reviews architecture for security and compliance controls

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Risk identification and mitigation planning
- Technical decision logs and design documentation
- Support during execution phase for complex technical issues

---

## Communications/Marketing Lead

### Role Summary
Communications/Marketing Leads manage internal and external communications about the project. They ensure consistent messaging, coordinate stakeholder updates, support change management, and enable marketing and launch activities that drive awareness and adoption.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and coordinate internal and external communications
- Create project updates, announcements, and documentation
- Manage stakeholder communication and engagement
- Support change management and user adoption initiatives
- Coordinate marketing and launch communications
- Maintain consistency in project messaging and branding

### Goals
- Enable stakeholder alignment and engagement
- Support user awareness and adoption of delivered solutions
- Maintain clear, consistent project communications
- Enhance project visibility and stakeholder buy-in

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Coordinates with Project Manager**: Aligns communication plans with project milestones and status
- **Works with Product Manager**: Supports product launch and user adoption messaging
- **Engages Stakeholders**: Provides regular updates and ensures stakeholder communications
- **Collaborates with Developers**: Translates technical achievements into accessible communications
- **Supports QA Lead**: Communicates quality and testing updates to stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Project status updates and announcements
- Launch communications and marketing materials
- Internal team newsletters and updates
- Stakeholder briefings and transparency communications

---

## Finance/Resource Manager

### Role Summary
Finance/Resource Managers oversee budget management, resource allocation, and cost tracking for projects. They ensure projects have the resources they need, stay within budget constraints, and provide financial visibility to project leadership and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Manage project budgets and financial forecasts
- Allocate resources according to project priorities
- Track and report on project costs and spending
- Identify and manage budget and resource constraints
- Support resource planning during initiation and planning phases
- Provide financial insights for project decisions

### Goals
- Ensure optimal allocation of financial and human resources
- Maintain cost control and budget compliance
- Provide financial visibility for project decision-making
- Support organizational financial objectives

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- **Partners with Project Manager**: Provides budget management, resource constraints, and financial reporting
- **Collaborates during Planning Phase**: Supports resource allocation and budget estimation
- **Advises Product Manager**: Provides cost-benefit analysis and financial trade-offs
- **Works with Developers**: Tracks resource utilization and team capacity
- **Communicates with Leadership**: Reports project financial status and budget forecasts

### Typical Communication
- Budget planning and forecasting discussions
- Resource allocation planning and reviews
- Financial status reports and cost tracking
- Budget variance analysis and recommendations
